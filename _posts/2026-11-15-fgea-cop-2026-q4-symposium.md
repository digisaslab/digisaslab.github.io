The FGEA CoP 2026 Q4 Symposium will be held online on Thursday 10 December 2026, from 10:00 am to 12:30 pm. Registration is by invitation only. For enquiries about joining future symposiums, email [FGEA@uts.edu.au](mailto:FGEA@uts.edu.au).

## Published agenda

- **10:00:** Introduction and welcome, Professor Asif Gill and the FGEA CoP advisory committee chairs.
- **10:10:** "Large-Scale AI Software Architecture", Professor Rick Kazman, UHM; chaired by Professor Asif Gill, UTS.
- **10:40:** "How AI is changing Product Management", Marcin Lubonski, Audinate; chaired by Madhushi Bandara, UTS.
- **11:10:** Short break and changeover.
- **11:20:** "Sovereignty is an Inference Problem", Leonardo Borges, CBA; chaired by Professor Asif Gill, UTS.
- **11:50:** Short break and changeover.
- **12:00:** "17 Considerations for Building a Successful Community of Practice", Darryl Carr, HCA Advisory; chaired by Madhushi Bandara, UTS.

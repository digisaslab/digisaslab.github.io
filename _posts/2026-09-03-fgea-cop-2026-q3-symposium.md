The FGEA CoP 2026 Q3 Symposium was scheduled online for Thursday 24 September 2026, from 10:00 am to 12:30 pm. Registration was by invitation.

## Published agenda

- **10:00:** Introduction and welcome, Professor Asif Gill and the FGEA CoP advisory committee chairs.
- **10:10:** "Intelligent Architecture for Decision-Making", Professor Asif Gill, UTS; chaired by Dr Madhushi Bandara, UTS.
- **10:40:** "AI and Application Portfolio Management", Mobin Barati, LeanIX, SAP; chaired by Dr Madhushi Bandara, UTS.
- **11:10:** Short break and changeover.
- **11:20:** "Data Mesh: Solving the Root Cause, Not the Symptoms", Senthoor Punniamoorthy, Telstra. The session addresses domain ownership, computational composition and genuine demand in data mesh; chaired by Dr Madhushi Bandara, UTS.
- **11:50:** Short break and changeover.
- **12:00:** EA for AI Research Showcase, chaired by Professor Asif Gill, UTS: "Data Governance for Resource-Constrained Organisations with Knowledge Graphs and Agentic AI", Bushra Al Sulayyim; and "Context-aware privacy risk assessment for Agentic AI systems", Grace Billiris.
